# ADEQUATE — Pre-PRD Full Product Specification
Version 0.1 · October 11, 2026 · Concept specification, subject to LG's approval

## 1. Executive concept
ADEQUATE (Always Remember, Everyone Is Adequate) is a founder-led personal-development brand and cross-platform product. Its central proposition: intrinsic human worth does not depend on achievement, while growth, agency, responsibility, and meaningful action remain possible. LG is its founder, primary voice, author, and creative director. This first enterprise precedes separate music and Substrate research enterprises.

## 2. Primary audience and value
Visitors discover LG's story and philosophy. Members use reflective tools, audio, books, exercises, journaling, and optional community to turn ideas into personally chosen action. LG can propose and approve brand/product direction in conversational language rather than technical tickets. The engineer receives structured, traceable implementation proposals.

## 3. Brand principles
- People are adequate before earning approval; never sell human worth or score it.
- Self-acceptance is compatible with growth and accountability.
- The present is a place of agency; past/future are useful contexts.
- The founder's real voice and exact words are distinct from editorial interpretation or AI-generated copy.
- Symbolism can be luminous, mysterious, geometric, and musical without suggesting secret affiliations or making unverifiable claims.
- LG's faith-based philosophy may appear in his authentic founder story; member exercises must remain welcoming to different beliefs.
- Respect user autonomy; avoid manipulative streaks, shame, false therapy claims, or AI impersonation.

## 4. Core product surfaces
### Public website
Hero and manifesto; LG introduction; founder story; ADEQUATE philosophy; audio/voice excerpts; product experience previews; library/book previews; workshops/community; transparent membership pricing; FAQ; accessibility, privacy, and contact pages.
### Member web and native apps
Today (daily check-in); Adequacy Mirror (strengths/assumptions/choices reflection); Pathways (decision exploration); Voice to Purpose (spoken reflection); Living Book (annotated reading and exercises); Convergence (pattern timeline and evidence vs interpretation); Adequate Together (opt-in moderated community). Shared account and history across desktop/tablet/mobile web, iOS, and Android.
### Founder Studio
Voice-first idea capture; original audio and transcript; interpretation and intent; concept clustering; candidate product/content implications; side-by-side previews; gentle, non-leading feedback prompts; explicit approvals; version history; publish gates; author attribution. Silence is not approval.
### Developer review
Structured proposals with provenance, affected surfaces, acceptance criteria, approval status, and implementation traceability. LG's conceptual direction is preserved, while technical work remains separate.

## 5. Content and publishing
LG-approved essays, first motivational book, chapters, narrated originals, audio reflections, podcasts, guided programs, workshops, and optional speaking engagements. Preserve recording masters, releases, transcripts, rights, and consent. AI-generated voice only with specific informed permission and clear labeling. Never invent founder quotations.

## 6. Business model hypotheses
Free: selected recordings, daily reflection, basic journal, limited AI usage. Plus proposed $8/month: advanced tools, saved history, voice workflows, premium programs. Circle proposed $19/month: Plus plus workshops and community programming. Additional book/audio purchases, speaking, group programs, and organizational licenses. Prices are exploratory and need validation; track gross vs net margin and service costs. Provide hardship access and avoid gating basic affirmation of worth.

## 7. AI strategy
A provider-neutral orchestration interface selects deterministic logic, licensed local models, authorized free-tier inference, or paid fallbacks by task, consent, privacy, quality, and cost. Structured schema validation, provenance, rate limiting, safe failure, and data deletion are mandatory for future implementation. Consumer AI webpages or WebViews are not assumed to be programmatic free APIs. Muse can be an optional conversational client only where integrations and permissions exist; native Founder Studio must stand alone.

## 8. Proposed implementation direction (not authorized in design phase)
TypeScript monorepo; Next.js responsive web; React Native/Expo iOS and Android; shared design tokens and domain schemas; PostgreSQL/Supabase for production data/auth; object storage for recordings; payment providers as platform policies require; modular AI router; event-based continuity; permissions, privacy, moderation, accessibility, analytics, and publishing workflows.

## 9. Design language and nomenclature
Brand: ADEQUATE. Expansion: Always Remember, Everyone Is Adequate. Proposed concepts: Adequacy Principle, Geometry of Growth, Possibility Fields, Architecture of Becoming, Present Moment, Resonance, Paths, Reflections, Circles. These are editorial/product metaphors and should not be presented as proven mathematics or proprietary scientific results. Use LG's own language wherever possible, subject to his approval.

## 10. Visual direction
Premium editorial art direction rather than generic wellness SaaS: human portraiture; expressive type; warm ink/ivory; charcoal; restrained luminous gold/amber; optional midnight indigo; geometric diagrams; subtle radiance and audio-wave forms. Mystical/symbolic influences as aesthetic vocabulary (halos, prisms, constellations, symmetry, circles, thresholds), never as claims of secret-society affiliation. Strong legibility, inclusive imagery, reduced-motion options, high contrast.

## 11. Design-only phase and approval criteria
First deliverable is an interactive visual concept studio: 3 genuinely distinct directions, comparative board, per-screen A/B variations, responsive desktop/tablet/mobile web and React Native iOS/Android design mockups, simulated interactions and animations, mock content only, and a persistent local-only approval ledger exportable to JSON/Markdown. LG can approve/reject/comment by concept, feature, screen, and detail. No backend, authentication, payment processing, live LLM, scraping, or deployment of production services. Final approved decisions become the engineering handoff.

## 12. Ownership, ethics, and decisions pending
Confirm LG's legal/brand name, public biography, recording/likeness rights, written consent, trademark availability, accessibility needs, target audience and age policy, revenue and intellectual-property agreements, launch pricing, content approval, and claims review. Distinguish direct quotes, paraphrases, placeholders, and hypotheses. Do not imply nonprofit status or charitable tax deductibility without verification. LG described the venture as a nonprofit in an early recording; legal status must be verified before public representation.

## 13. Success measures
Qualitative: LG recognizes his voice in the design, feels comfortable challenging concepts, and approves a coherent brand direction. User: visitor understands proposition quickly; member can complete one useful reflection without payment. Business: conversion and retention without manipulative incentives; sustainable gross margin. Technical later: privacy, uptime, accessibility, and reliable cross-platform continuity.

## 14. Out of scope for first design milestone
Real business logic, databases, live API integrations, payment processing, real member data, app-store submission, published founder quotes without review, and production infrastructure.
